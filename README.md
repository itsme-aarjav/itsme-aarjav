## About Me

I am a B.Tech CSE (AI and ML) graduate focused on **DevOps, Cloud Engineering, and DevSecOps**.

Most of my learning has come from hands-on work: setting up AWS infrastructure, working with Linux, building Docker images, creating CI/CD pipelines, deploying workloads on Kubernetes and Amazon EKS, automating infrastructure with Terraform, and troubleshooting things when they did not work the first time.

I enjoy understanding how different parts of a production environment connect together, from source control and CI/CD to infrastructure, containers, security, monitoring, and recovery.

Currently focused on building **production-oriented DevOps and Cloud Engineering projects** and improving my understanding of real-world infrastructure and deployment workflows.
